Self reflection identity and future wishes

Deep self reflection helps explain why a memory, preference or hope matters. Onboarding records the first account; later reflection explores its meaning and allows the person to revise it. The conversation follows their interest, language and energy, with a chosen supporter or an appropriately qualified professional where relevant. [P4]

Explore the meaning behind an answer

Someone saying they miss home may mean a garden, privacy, neighbours, a view or the freedom to choose when to go outside. Exploring that meaning gives supporters something practical to respond to. The recording links the person's explanation with its circumstances, while a proposed interpretation is returned for correction.

Questions also reach beyond immediate care. What makes a satisfying day? Which experiences changed an opinion? What would the person like to learn? How do they want relationships to develop? Their answers help an Aura retain a developing account of values and intentions, rather than only a history of appointments and problems.

Public private and secret selves

The design uses public, private and secret selves to describe different contexts of expression and access. Public material is what the person chooses to express outwardly. Private material holds context for personal use and selected relationships. Secret material describes especially intimate or closely held information with more specific boundaries. [P4, page 5]

These distinctions help explain why a short public introduction and a trusted private conversation contain different detail. Recording something internally does not itself publish it. Proposed sharing, model training and research uses are explained for the information involved and the audience or purpose being considered.

A past that informs an unfolding life

A recollection stays identifiable as a recollection, with uncertainty and other accounts kept visible. Reflection invites understanding without pressing the person towards a preferred answer. Sensitive topics receive an appropriate pace and support, and an encounter pauses when it becomes unwelcome or distressing.

Future wishes might concern who the person wants involved, how they prefer help to be offered, places they hope to visit or something they want to create. Formal legal instructions follow the relevant legal process. A personal recording adds context to those discussions and remains distinguishable from a legally effective document. [R25]

Humour, creativity and ordinary enjoyment also belong here. The developing record has room for what the person finds delightful now and what they would like to explore next. That makes self reflection relevant to living, as well as to remembering.

A useful next step

Begin with something that matters

A simple question can open a useful conversation: when you say you miss home, which part of home do you mean? The person’s explanation leads the next step.
