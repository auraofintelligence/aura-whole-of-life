The Protopian purpose

Raise capability. Keep the person in charge.

Aura’s ambition is a fair go as intelligence becomes more capable: people developing the understanding, tools and judgement to shape their own lives. Younger adults, older people and those whose needs change belong in that future, with room for creativity, pleasure, learning and relationships.

Ethical engagement means being able to examine a source, question an interpretation, change a record and choose what an assistant may share or do. The proposed human and twin partnership develops through that continuing exchange.

Protopian improvement begins with something useful, tests its effects and learns from the people involved. Personal dignity, accountable support and wider access to capability give the technological ambition its purpose.

The introduction

Support that can grow with a person

The first step is a useful, recognisable account of a life. The next step is learning whether it helps in an actual encounter.

Aura of Intelligence is being developed as a personal artificial intelligence (AI) environment that grows with a person throughout life. Its proposed digital twin is a computer representation of selected aspects of a person's body, mind and life, built from chosen records and their meaning. The purpose is adaptive whole of life support: help that remains relevant as health, circumstances and priorities change. [P2, P7-P8]

This introduction concerns adults living with younger onset dementia, adults receiving disability support, and people beginning or increasing aged care support. It is also for family, friends, paid and unpaid carers, registered providers, clinicians and government agencies considering where Aura might fit.

If a person chooses to begin, onboarding is the guided starting process. It records a minimum viable self: the agreed personal information needed for the first useful functions, such as preparing a care introduction. It establishes identity, communication, relationships, values and relevant circumstances. The person's own words, corrections and unanswered questions give that first record its meaning. [P4]

The intended benefits follow from that foundation. A person has a familiar way to express a preference, organise a meaningful activity or preserve the connections between a place and their life. Supporters receive relevant context, while practitioners bring their own assessments and responsibilities. These are benefits the programme seeks to demonstrate through practical use and research.

The present stage of development

There is a public website, substantial design work and a working browser prototype, Aura Matrix Studio. This early version offers personal questions, editable records, visual organisation, saving on the device and downloadable copies. A separate clinical plan describes dementia research and development of medical software. Secure integration, the complete care service and evidence of clinical benefit remain work to complete. [P3, P7-P8]

The following pages explain the proposed experience, its intended benefits and how it would be tested and delivered responsibly. Verse Box is a proposed public computer station for a guided introduction to personal Aura tools. Demonstrations offer an opportunity to examine them before considering personal onboarding. Interest creates no obligation to participate; any offer needs clear terms and an identifiable responsible organisation. [P2]

A practical beginning

Choose a purpose. Try it. Review it.

  1. Choose one goal

    A care introduction, an activity or a question worth preparing.

  2. Record enough to begin

    Personal context, chosen sources and unanswered questions.

  3. Try something useful

    Use the account in the situation it was prepared for.

  4. Review and correct

    Keep what helped and change what did not fit.

Memories and meaningful places

A home is more than an address

A garden path, a kitchen shelf or a favourite chair can lead into a story. Preparing a small collection before downsizing gives those connections a way to travel into the next home.

Photographs and recordings join the person’s own explanation. A printed album or an ordinary conversation may be the right format.

An everyday route and a separate research proposal

Understand what is being offered

Ordinary Aura support begins with a chosen use. The intensive adult Genesis design brings HBOT and Truth Engine research into a separate proposed experience. It is research in development; it does not establish a validated Aura treatment or lie-detection service.
