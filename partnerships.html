A practical development and partnership strategy

The next stage turns a defined use into a concrete proposal for the people and organisations involved. The starting question is which part of life the person wants supported. The minimum viable self gives that work a personal foundation; the delivery plan identifies the people, evidence, resources and approvals needed around it.

Prepare an experience that is clear enough to review

A demonstration uses a small example and an understandable explanation of current functions. It shows recording, finding and correcting meaningful information. Sensitive personal material is introduced only through suitable arrangements. The person sees the intended use and the limits of the particular version being offered.

A local proposal then names the intended participants, the activity, the accountable provider and the clinical or research roles relevant to it. It sets out equipment, accessibility, staff and family time, information handling, transport where needed and a realistic budget. Costs and proposed funding are visible before an invitation to participate.

Establish readiness before recruitment

The clinical plan, ethics and site requirements, lawful consent, privacy controls and appropriate therapeutic goods pathway are settled for the actual study. Roles and agreements identify who responds to a concern and who maintains continuity. These are work to complete, rather than assumptions supplied by the existence of a public prototype. [P3]

A focused first evaluation records whether onboarding produces a recognisable and usable starting self, whether the chosen activity offers value, and what burden or unwanted effects arise. Findings inform design changes and the next study decision. Clinical software and interventions progress through their respective evidence and regulatory processes.

An understandable next conversation

For an individual or family, the next conversation begins with a goal, preferred communication and questions about what is actually available. A carer or registered provider identifies where continuity or a meaningful activity might help. A clinician, research partner or agency considers a defined role and the evidence relevant to its responsibility.

The full direction remains adaptive whole of life support. The person begins with a reviewed account of themselves and develops it through living. The programme develops through the same discipline: an explicit purpose, a practical experience, a careful review and a reasoned next step.
