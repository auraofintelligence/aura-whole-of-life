Primary-source checks · 28 October 2026

Current context alongside the dated proposal

The checks below cover specific statements. The complete source text remains the author’s dated proposal; these checks do not establish Aura’s clinical benefit, regulatory approval or funding eligibility.

Software intended for a medical purpose

TGA guidance explains that intended purpose determines whether software is a medical device. Regulated software generally needs ARTG inclusion before supply unless excluded or exempt. This does not classify or approve Aura.

TGA: understanding software-based medical devices ↗ (opens in a new tab)

NDIS age and transition rules

The NDIS page states that applicants must be younger than 65 when applying. Existing participants can choose to continue after 65, with a separate requirement to leave when moving into residential aged care after 65. Other eligibility and item-funding rules also apply.

NDIS: what are the age requirements ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Assessed support at home

My Aged Care confirms Support at Home began in November 2025. Services and funding follow assessment and approval. Its AT-HM scheme separately funds approved assistive technology and home modifications for assessed needs; this is not approval of an Aura product.

My Aged Care: Support at Home ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Assistive technology and home modifications

The AT-HM scheme page describes an aged care assessment, an approval recorded in the person’s decision letter and support plan, and provider involvement for relevant equipment or modifications.

My Aged Care: AT-HM scheme ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Specialist hyperbaric delivery

RBWH describes specialist fitness assessment, qualified medical, nursing and technical delivery, permitted-item restrictions and clinical referral. Its service information does not establish a benefit for the proposed combined Aura programme.

Royal Brisbane and Women’s Hospital: Hyperbaric Medicine ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Project sources

Project sources describe intentions and development status. Research and official guidance provide separate evidence and context. A citation identifies a source for a statement, not a partnership, endorsement or approval of Aura. Sources were reviewed on 25 October 2026, with selected public references checked again on 27 October 2026. P identifies project material; R identifies research or public guidance.
